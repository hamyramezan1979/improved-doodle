# Executive Summary: "The Last Migration"
## Five Professional Perspectives for Hamy Ramezan

## 🎯 WHAT THE FILM IS ABOUT

**"The Last Migration"** is an intimate character study following Amir, a Middle Eastern refugee navigating cultural integration in Finland. The story explores the mutual adaptation required when cultures meet, using the metaphor of environmental change (forest logging) to represent the tension between tradition and progress.

**Core Themes**:
- **Identity & Belonging**: What does "home" mean when you're between cultures?
- **Mutual Integration**: How both immigrants and host communities must adapt
- **Environmental Metaphor**: Change vs. preservation in both nature and culture
- **Human Connection**: Communication beyond language barriers
- **Resilience**: Finding strength through cultural exchange
