# Sample Script: "The Last Migration"

## FADE IN:

**EXT. FINNISH FOREST - DAWN**

*Snow covers the ancient pines. The silence is broken by the distant sound of heavy machinery. AMIR (35), a Middle Eastern refugee, walks through the forest carrying a worn backpack. His breath forms clouds in the cold air.*

**AMIR (V.O.)**
They say Finland is the land of a thousand lakes. What they don't tell you is that it's also the land of a thousand silences.

*He stops at a clearing where a modern logging operation is cutting down centuries-old trees. Workers in bright safety gear operate machinery. The contrast is stark.*

**INT. REFUGEE CENTER - KITCHEN - DAY**

*Amir prepares traditional Middle Eastern food while other refugees from various countries work around him. SOFIA (28), a Finnish social worker, enters.*

**SOFIA**
(in English, with Finnish accent)
The integration interview is in an hour. Are you ready?

**AMIR**
(stirring a pot)
I've been ready for three years. The question is whether Finland is ready for me.

*Sofia notices the rich aromas filling the kitchen.*

**SOFIA**
What are you making?

**AMIR**
Memories. My grandmother's recipe. The only thing that survived the journey.

**EXT. LAKE SAIMAA - SUNSET**

*Amir sits alone on a dock, looking across the vast lake. His phone rings - it's a call from back home. He stares at it but doesn't answer.*

**AMIR (V.O.)**
In my language, there's a word - "ghorba." It means the pain of being away from home. But what do you call the pain of not knowing where home is anymore?

*A Finnish fisherman, EINO (60s), approaches with his catch.*

**EINO**
(in broken English)
Fish... good today. You... hungry?

*Amir looks up, surprised by the kindness. For the first time, he smiles.*

**AMIR**
Yes. I am hungry.

**FADE TO BLACK.**

**THE END**

---

## Script Analysis Framework

This is a 15-page feature film concept that explores themes of:
- Cultural integration and identity
- Environmental change and tradition
- Human connection across language barriers
- The immigrant experience in Nordic countries
- The intersection of ancient traditions and modern life
